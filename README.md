## 🚀 About Me

I am a physicist working at the intersection of **biomedical optics** and **computational imaging**. As a PhD candidate at TU Delft, I am developing **high-resolution optical coherence tomography (OCT)** for **skin cancer diagnostics**, covering both the optical hardware and the reconstruction methods behind it.

* **Current Focus:** High-resolution OCT, spectrometer design, and computational image reconstruction.
* **Previous:** Erasmus Mundus LASCALA Master's — 🇫🇷 Paris-Saclay · 🇭🇺 Szeged · 🇸🇪 Lund. Thesis: [*Feasibility of Adult Lung Monitoring Using GASMAS: Development and Evaluation of Advanced Large-Geometry Phantoms*](https://lup.lub.lu.se/student-papers/search/publication/9236328) (Lund University, 2026).
* **Background:** BSc (Hons) in Physics, University of Colombo, Sri Lanka.

## 💻 Research & Simulations

### **🩺 Optical Coherence Tomography (PhD)**
* **System Design:** Spectral-domain OCT at 1.3 µm, including spectrometer design in **Zemax OpticStudio**.
* **Computational Imaging:** OCT signal processing and depth reconstruction in **Python**.

### **🫁 Tissue Optics & GASMAS (Master's)**
* **Monte Carlo Simulations:** GPU-accelerated photon transport in layered tissue geometries using **pmcx/MCX** for GASMAS feasibility studies.
* **Laser Absorption Spectroscopy:** Wavelength modulation spectroscopy with lock-in detection and **HITRAN/HAPI** spectral modelling.

### **🔬 Spectroscopy & Data Analysis**
* **Spectroscopy & Raman Analysis:** Processing and automated analysis of spectroscopic datasets using **Pandas & SciPy**.
* **Hyperspectral Imaging:** Analysis of remote sensing data with custom algorithms.
* **Chemometrics:** Multivariate data analysis (SVD/PCA) and spectral decomposition for optical datasets.

### **⚡ Ultrafast & Femtosecond Optics**
* **Laser Pulse Simulation:** Modeling femtosecond pulse propagation and nonlinear light-matter interactions using **Python/MATLAB**.
